# India Legal & Regulatory Compliance Review Checklist
## User-Generated Film Industry Rumors & Gossip Platform

> **Disclaimer**: This checklist serves as an engineering compliance blueprint. All policies, Terms of Service, Privacy Policies, and Grievance redressal workflows MUST be reviewed by qualified legal counsel in India prior to public deployment.

---

## 1. Statutory & Regulatory Framework

### A. Information Technology Act, 2000 & IT Intermediary Rules, 2021
- [x] **Safe Harbor Immunity (Section 79)**: Maintain intermediary status by not initiating the transmission, selecting the receiver, or modifying published user content.
- [x] **Grievance Officer Designation**: Publish the name, email, and postal address of the designated Grievance Redressal Officer in India with acknowledgement within 24 hours and resolution within 15 days.
- [x] **Notice & Takedown Protocol**: Support expedited content removal/disablement within 36 hours of receipt of a valid court order or notification by authorized government agencies under Rule 3(1)(d).
- [x] **Automated Due Diligence**: Automated pre-publish screening for PII (Aadhaar, PAN, phone numbers, home addresses) and sexualized content involving minors.
- [x] **Monthly Compliance Reports**: Mechanism to log total reports received, content actions taken, and appeal outcomes for monthly transparency reporting.

### B. Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP)
- [x] **Consent & Purpose Limitation**: Clear user consent modal during account registration for processing display name, email, and user-generated posts.
- [x] **Right to Erasure & Correction**: Provide user settings to delete account, erase uploaded rumors/comments, or correct profile details.
- [x] **Children Data Protection**: Hard-block policy on gathering or publishing personal identifiers or sexualized content of individuals under 18 years.

### C. Copyright Act, 1957
- [x] **Copyright Infringement Reporting**: Dedicated copyright takedown request form for movie footage, leaked screenplays, posters, or audio tracks.
- [x] **Repeat Infringer Policy**: Progressive account restrictions (warning -> posting block -> account termination) for repeat copyright infringers.

### D. Criminal & Civil Defamation (Sections 499/500 IPC / Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita)
- [x] **Unverified Content Disclaimers**: Prominent visual labels (`UNVERIFIED — FAN RUMOR`, `SPECULATION — NOT CONFIRMED`) preventing rumors from being represented as confirmed factual statements.
- [x] **User Responsibility Declaration**: Mandatory pre-publishing checkbox requiring users to confirm their post is unverified speculation/opinion.
- [x] **High-Risk Claim Quarantine**: Automatic flagging of allegations involving criminal activity, drug use, or sexual misconduct for Trust & Safety queue review.
